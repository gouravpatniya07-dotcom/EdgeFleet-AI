Edge-AI Distributed Coordination Protocol

Unlike legacy automated guided vehicles (AGVs) that rely on a central server with single points of failure, this system runs full decentralized peer-to-peer (P2P) negotiation directly on edge microcontrollers.

How It Works:

1. Space-Time Horizon Prediction: Each AMR computes its projected trajectory vector [(x0, y0, t0), ..., (xn, yn, tn)] up to 5 seconds into the future based on its kinematic constraints.
2. P2P Neighborhood Broadcast: AMRs broadcast their space-time horizons over local ad-hoc radio (DSRC/C-V2X) to any neighbor within their 180px communication horizon.
3. Deterministic Priority Deconfliction: When two horizons overlap within clearance distance, both AMRs calculate the same deterministic priority ranking:
• Emergency/Critical Battery: Priority 5
• High-Priority Express Cargo: Priority 4
• Loaded Transport: Priority 3
• Returning / Empty: Priority 2
• Tie-Breaker: Deterministic AMR-ID hash.
The lower-priority AMR proactively yields or slows down at the intersection entrance.
4. Dynamic Local Obstacle Avoidance: On-board 2D LIDAR sensors continuously detect unknown obstacles (spills/dropped pallets) and dynamically invoke local A* replanning without contacting any central controller.
